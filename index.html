About

I study disease where it actually happens: in living organisms. Working with Swiss albino mice and C. elegans, I generate in vivo evidence, then use network pharmacology, docking, molecular dynamics and machine learning to explain why it works and decide what to test next.

🐭Swiss albino mouse

  • Diabetic mouse model: stevioside and metformin synergy (MSc thesis)
  • Handling, dissection and histopathology
  • Findings tied to in silico target and pathway analysis

🪱C. elegans

  • Lifespan and fertility assays with indigenous yogurt probiotics
  • Research Assistant maintaining worm cultures, 2023–2024
  • Best Oral Presenter, ICALS 2025

I have co-authored 16 journal articles, reviewed manuscripts for 14 international journals, and mentor undergraduate students in bioinformatics research.
